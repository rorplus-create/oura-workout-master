Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

1. Overview

Workout Master is a personal fitness and recovery tracking application intended for use by its owner.

2. Data Collected

With the user's authorization, the application may access Oura sleep, readiness, activity, heart rate, and workout data.

3. Purpose

Data is used to review recovery trends and support daily exercise planning.

4. Storage and Sharing

The application is designed to store collected data locally on the owner's computer. It does not intentionally publish health data or share it with third parties.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

The owner controls locally stored data and may delete it at any time.

6. Security

API credentials and health data must not be committed to public source repositories.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated if the application's data handling practices change.
